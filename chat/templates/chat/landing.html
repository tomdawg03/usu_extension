Your local extension expert, always available. The USU Extension AI Assistant is a conversational tool built in partnership with USU Analytics Solutions Center. It connects you with credible, locally relevant answers drawn from thousands of USU fact sheets, so you spend less time searching and more time doing.

How it works

USU has nearly 15,000 research-based fact sheets across more than 100 webpages — finding the accurate information you need can be challenging. AGNES stores all of the fact sheets in one convenient location and uses abstractive summarization to answer your questions. No hallucinations, only research-backed answers with direct sources for your reference.

Tip: The more specific your question, the better AGNES can match it to the right fact sheets. Try including details like your crop type, region, or specific problem.
